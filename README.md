About the Certification
The DataCamp Data Analyst Certification is a rigorous assessment of data analysis skills. It covers data cleaning, exploratory analysis, visualization, communication of insights, and making data-driven recommendations. This project was completed as part of the certification process.

📊 Project Overview
Business Goal:
Analyze sales data to identify which sales methods are most effective and provide recommendations to optimize revenue and resource allocation.

Key Steps:

Cleaned and prepared the dataset for analysis

Explored revenue distribution and sales method frequency

Defined and used a key metric: revenue per sales method

Visualized patterns and trends in the data

Delivered business-focused insights and recommendations

💡 Key Findings
Email + Call is the most effective sales method in terms of revenue

Email performs well as a cost-efficient and consistent channel

Call should be targeted toward high-potential clients

Higher product volume sold correlates with higher revenue
